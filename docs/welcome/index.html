Bills and income

Budget Helper

See what is left to spend once your regular bills are paid, however often they go out and whatever currency they are in.

Open Budget Helper How to install

Free. No account, no tracking. It runs in your browser.

How it works

  1. 1

    Add your income

    Enter what comes in and how often: weekly, fortnightly, monthly, or every few weeks or months.

  2. 2

    Add your bills

    Rent, power, subscriptions: each with its amount, how often it goes out, and the date and timezone it goes out in.

  3. 3

    Read your budget

    Everything is turned into a year, then shown per week, fortnight, month or year. $10,000 a month is $120,000 a year, and a fortnightly bill goes out 26 times.

What else it does

  • Bills in other currencies. Give a bill its own currency and a conversion rate, and it is counted in your income currency.
  • Before your next pay. See the bills due before your next pay, and what that pay leaves after the bills that follow it.
  • Reminders. Once a day, at the hour you choose, one notification lists the bills that go out in the next 24 hours, wherever they are.
  • Light and dark. It follows your phone's setting.

Your data stays on your phone

There is no account to make. Your income and bills are stored in the browser on your device. If you turn on reminders, only the reminder wording and times go to your reminder server. Back up to a file from Settings to move to another device.

Install it

Installed, it opens full screen like an app and works without a connection.

iPhone and iPad
Open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
Android
Open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
Mac
In Safari, choose File, then Add to Dock. In Chrome, use the install button in the address bar.
